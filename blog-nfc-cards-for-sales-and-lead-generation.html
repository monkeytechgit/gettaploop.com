NFC Cards for Sales: How to Capture More Leads

How to use NFC business cards for sales: profiles with a clear call to action, contact capture, fast follow-up, and metrics for sales teams.

In this article
  1. A Card as Your Starting Point
  2. Design Your Profile to Convert
  3. Capture Contacts
  4. Fast Follow-Up
  5. Metrics for Your Team
  6. A Consistent Brand Across Your Team

A Card as Your Starting Point

In sales, the first contact is just the beginning of a process. An NFC card makes that first step instant: in seconds, the prospect has your information and a way to respond.

Design Your Profile to Convert

Put your WhatsApp button or a "schedule a call" button near the top. Add a short catalog or pitch, and avoid cluttering your profile with too many links.

Capture Contacts

When your profile lets people leave their information, every interaction can turn into a lead. That way, you're not only relying on the prospect reaching out to you first.

Fast Follow-Up

Follow up within the first 24 to 48 hours, mentioning the context of your conversation. A short, personalized message works better than a generic one.

Metrics for Your Team

Tracking visits and clicks helps you see which links are working and compare results across events or sales reps.

A Consistent Brand Across Your Team

With a shared design and one profile per rep, everyone represents the same brand, and you manage updates from a single, centralized place.

FAQ

Does it work at trade shows and conferences?

Yes, it speeds up exchanging contact information when there's a crowd.

Can I see who visited my profile?

It depends on your profile settings — just ask about this when you request your quote.
