What to Put on Your NFC Digital Profile

Ideas for what to include on your NFC card profile: contact info, WhatsApp, social media, portfolio, location, and a call to action that gets you more contacts.

In this article
  1. Start With the Essentials
  2. Direct Contact Buttons
  3. Save Contact Button
  4. Social Media and Website
  5. Portfolio, Catalog, or Booking Link
  6. Location and Hours
  7. A Call to Action

Start With the Essentials

Name, job title or specialty, company, and a photo or logo. These should all be clearly visible on a phone screen.

Direct Contact Buttons

Call, WhatsApp, and email, all with a single tap. Every extra step reduces the odds that someone will actually reach out.

Save Contact Button

Let people add you to their contacts instantly, so they don't have to rely on remembering your name.

Social Media and Website

Include only the networks you actually use professionally, such as LinkedIn, Instagram, or your website.

Portfolio, Catalog, or Booking Link

A link to your work, your catalog, or a calendar for booking meetings turns a simple card into a sales tool.

Location and Hours

If you see clients in person, a map and your hours make it easy for them to find you.

A Call to Action

Close with a clear line: "Book a Call" or "Request a Quote." Fewer options mean more clarity.

FAQ

How many links should I include?

Only as many as you need. Prioritize the ones that lead to an action, and avoid overloading the profile.

Can I change my profile later?

Yes, the profile is editable and the card keeps working.
