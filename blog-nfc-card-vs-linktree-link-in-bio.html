NFC Card vs. Linktree and Link-in-Bio Tools

The differences between an NFC business card and a Linktree-style link-in-bio tool: in-person contact, brand image, reach, and when to use each one.

In this article
  1. What is a link-in-bio tool
  2. What an NFC card adds
  3. They complement each other
  4. When to choose each one
  5. Recommendation

What is a link-in-bio tool

It's a page with multiple links that you add to your social media profile. It helps direct your online audience to your content.

What an NFC card adds

It leads to a similar profile, but from the physical world. Someone meets you in person, and with a single tap they land on your information.

They complement each other

A digital profile can include all your social media links. The NFC card acts as the bridge between an in-person conversation and your digital presence.

When to choose each one

If your audience is mostly on social media, a link-in-bio tool may be enough. If you do networking, in-person sales, or client visits, an NFC card gives you an instant way in.

Recommendation

Use both: the link-in-bio tool for your social media, and the NFC card for meetings, trade shows, and clients.

FAQ

Can I use my current link on the card?

Yes, the card can open whatever link or profile you set up.

Which one is better for business?

For in-person interactions and sales, the NFC card adds more value.
