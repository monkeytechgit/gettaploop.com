Metal NFC Cards as a Corporate Gift

Custom metal NFC cards make a practical, memorable corporate gift. Ideas for using them with clients, executives, events, and welcoming new team members.

In this article

Why It Works as a Gift

Unlike a promotional item that ends up forgotten in a drawer, an NFC card actually gets used. It also communicates quality and attention to detail.

Ideal Occasions

  • Welcoming new team members.
  • Recognitions and work anniversaries.
  • Gifts for key clients.
  • Kits for events and conferences.

Personalization

You can engrave a name, logo, or message, and set up a personal or company profile that opens to your website or catalog.

When to Choose Metal

A metal finish, like matte black, works especially well for executives and high-level clients. For larger volumes, PVC can be more practical.

How to Request a Quote

Tell us the quantity, material, design, and what information the card should open. We'll use that to put together a custom proposal.

FAQ

Can each card be engraved with a different name?

Yes, each card can be personalized individually — just mention it when requesting your quote.

Minimum quantities are confirmed during the quote process.
