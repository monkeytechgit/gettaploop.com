NFC Card or QR Code: When to Use Each

NFC and QR codes do the same job with different technology. Learn how they differ, the advantages of each, and why TapLoop cards combine both to share your profile.

In this article
  1. Two ways to reach the same link
  2. NFC: just tap and go
  3. QR: universal and visible
  4. Why combine them
  5. Our recommendation

Two ways to reach the same link

Both NFC and QR codes take someone to a web address — in this case, your digital profile. The difference is in how it's read.

NFC: just tap and go

Just tap the card against the phone. There's no need to open the camera or focus on anything. It's fast and makes a great first impression.

QR: universal and visible

The QR code is scanned with the camera. It works as a backup if the phone doesn't have NFC or has it turned off, and it can be shown on a screen or printed.

Why combine them

Having both on the same card covers every situation without depending on a single method. That's why TapLoop cards include both NFC and a QR code.

Our recommendation

You don't have to choose. Use NFC when you're face-to-face with someone, and the QR code when you're sharing on screen, in printed material, or with older phones.

FAQ

Does the QR code need internet?

To open the profile, yes — the phone needs an internet connection.

Which one is faster?

Usually NFC, since it doesn't require opening the camera.
