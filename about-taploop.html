Who TapLoop Is

A More Modern Way to Present Your Information

TapLoop transforms the traditional business card into a reusable digital tool. Each physical card can open a digital profile with your information, contact channels, social media, website, catalog, portfolio, and other links.

The information is shared through NFC technology or a QR code, without the other person having to install an app.

Our Specialty

Custom NFC Digital Business Cards

  • Programmed NFC chip that opens the digital profile.
  • QR code linked to the same profile.
  • Design aligned with your logo and visual identity.
  • Digital profile with your info, WhatsApp, social media, and links.

Our Purpose

Making Professional Connections That Actually Last

An effective introduction doesn't end when you hand someone a card. That's why we bring the important information together in a digital profile that's easy to open, save, and revisit after a meeting, visit, event, or business conversation.

Our goal is to help professionals and businesses communicate more clearly who they are, what they offer, and how they can be reached.

FAQ

About TapLoop and Its NFC Cards

Answers about our company, digital profiles, and NFC presentation solutions.

What is TapLoop? +

TapLoop creates NFC digital business cards and custom digital profiles for professionals, teams, and businesses.

What information can a TapLoop digital profile include? +

It can include your name, job title, phone number, WhatsApp, email, website, social media, location, catalog, portfolio, and other relevant links.

Do TapLoop cards include NFC and a QR code? +

Yes. TapLoop solutions can integrate a programmed NFC chip and a QR code that both link to the same digital profile.

Does TapLoop create cards for businesses and teams? +

Yes. TapLoop develops a consistent visual identity and customizes the card and digital profile for each team member.

Does the other person need to install an app? +

No. The digital profile opens directly in the phone's browser via NFC or by scanning the QR code.

Does TapLoop offer metal NFC cards? +

Yes. TapLoop offers digital NFC cards in PVC and metal NFC cards for executive presentations or added impact.
