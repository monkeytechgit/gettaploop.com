Smart Cards: What They Are and What They're For

Smart business cards combine an NFC chip, QR code, and digital profile. Discover what they are, how they're used, and how they differ from other cards.

A term with several meanings

"Smart card" gets used for a lot of things: chip-based bank cards, access badges, or tech-enabled business cards. Here, we're talking about the latter.

The smart business card

It's a physical card with an NFC chip and QR code that opens your digital profile. It brings your contact info, social links, website, and more together in a single object.

What they're for

They let you share your information with a single tap, keep it up to date without reprinting, and offer a more modern experience than paper.

How they differ from other chip cards

TapLoop's NFC business cards don't process payments or store banking data — they simply open the link to your profile, which you control.

How to get one

Choose your material (PVC or metal), send us your design and details, and TapLoop will set up your profile and test the NFC and QR before shipping.

FAQ

Is a smart card the same as an NFC card?

In the context of business cards, yes — the card uses NFC to open your digital profile.

Does it store my personal data on the chip?

The chip stores the link to your profile; your information is managed in the digital profile itself.
