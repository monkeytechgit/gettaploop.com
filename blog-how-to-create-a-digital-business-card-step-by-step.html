How to Create Your Digital Business Card, Step by Step

Learn how to create your NFC digital business card: define your info, design your profile, choose a material, test NFC and QR, and share it with a single tap.

Step 1: Define What You'll Use It For

Before you design anything, get clear on your goal: winning new clients, making it easier to connect at events, or giving your team a unified look. That goal decides what information comes first.

Step 2: Gather Your Information and Links

Prepare your logo, a professional photo, phone number, WhatsApp, email, social media, and website. Double-check that every link works and that your information is written exactly the way you want it to appear.

Step 3: Choose a Material and Design

Decide between PVC and metal, and settle on colors, a logo, and a short tagline. A clean design with good contrast is easier to read and makes a stronger impression. Make the most of the space: less text, more clarity.

Step 4: Set Up Your Digital Profile

Order your buttons by priority: call, WhatsApp, save contact, then everything else. A well-organized profile can be understood in a few seconds on a phone screen.

Step 5: Test the Card

Before you start using it, make sure it opens correctly with NFC and with the QR code, on both iPhone and Android. TapLoop runs these tests before your card ships.

Step 6: Use It and Track Results

Share it at meetings and events. Check which links get the most engagement and fine-tune your profile over time.

Yes, the profile is editable and the card keeps working.
