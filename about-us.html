Our Purpose

Making Every Introduction More Professional and Simple

TapLoop was created to make it easier for a person or business to share their information. Instead of relying on printed cards, scattered messages, or hard-to-find links, we bring the most important information together in one NFC card and digital profile.

Our approach combines design, technology, and hands-on support so every solution is clear, useful, and consistent with each client's identity.

What We Do

Digital Business Cards Designed for Every Brand

  • Custom NFC cards in PVC and metal.
  • Digital profiles with contact info, WhatsApp, social media, and links.
  • Integrated QR code as an alternative way to access the profile.
  • Solutions for professionals, teams, and businesses.
