FAQ

Key answers about the Metal Digital NFC Card.

What's the difference between the metal and PVC NFC cards?+

The metal card offers more weight, presence, and a premium finish. The PVC option is lighter and more practical for everyday use or larger-volume rollouts.

Does the metal card include NFC and a QR code?+

Yes. It includes a programmed NFC chip and a QR code to open the same digital profile from compatible phones.

Can it be customized with my brand?+

Yes. The design is customized with your logo, name, title, QR code, and visual style, and the digital profile includes your info and links.

Can I update the digital profile later?+

Yes. Depending on your plan, you can update the digital profile's info and links without replacing the metal card.

Can I order metal cards for an executive team?+

Yes. We can create a corporate visual identity and customize each card and profile with the details of executives, partners, or key team members.

How long does delivery take?+

Estimated delivery is 3-5 business days after design and production are approved. Timing can vary by quantity and destination.
