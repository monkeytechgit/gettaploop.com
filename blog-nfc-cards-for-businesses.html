NFC Cards for Businesses: Why Teams Are Adopting Them

See how NFC business cards help sales and service teams: a consistent brand image, individual profiles for every team member, performance metrics, and centralized management.

In this article
  1. A common problem on teams
  2. One brand image, one profile per person
  3. Centralized management
  4. Capturing and tracking contacts
  5. How to get started

A common problem on teams

When every team member orders their own cards, your brand image gets fragmented and the information becomes inconsistent. Every time someone changes roles or phone numbers, the cards have to be reprinted.

One brand image, one profile per person

With corporate NFC cards, the whole team shares the same company design, and each team member has their own digital profile with their name, title, phone number, and links.

Centralized management

New hires, departures, and changes are all handled without reprinting a single card. If someone leaves the company, their profile can be reassigned or deactivated.

Capturing and tracking contacts

Profiles can help capture leads and track interactions, giving you visibility into what's working at trade shows, client visits, and networking events.

How to get started

Decide how many team members will get a card, which material (PVC or metal), and what information will appear on each profile. With those details, the TapLoop team will put together a proposal for you.

FAQ

Yes, each person has their own profile with their own information and links.
