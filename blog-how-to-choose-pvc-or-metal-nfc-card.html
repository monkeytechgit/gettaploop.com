How to Choose Between a PVC or Metal NFC Card

A guide to choosing between a PVC and a metal NFC card: durability, finish, image, everyday use, and budget for professionals and businesses.

In this article
  1. Two materials, two use profiles
  2. PVC NFC Card
  3. Metal NFC Card
  4. What to consider when deciding
  5. Design and profile

Two materials, two use profiles

Both options work the same way on a technical level: NFC chip, QR code, and an editable digital profile. The difference lies in the material, the finish, and the perception.

PVC NFC Card

PVC is flexible, lightweight, and allows full-color printing with your own design. It's ideal for everyday use, for outfitting entire teams, and for anyone looking for a good balance between quality and budget.

Metal NFC Card

The metal version offers a premium finish, like matte black with laser engraving. It's a great option for executives, premium service professionals, and corporate gifts.

What to consider when deciding

Think about how many cards you need, who will receive them, and what image you want to convey. For many teams, PVC for general staff and metal for leadership is a practical combination.

Design and profile

Whatever material you choose, make sure the design stays true to your brand and that your digital profile is complete before printing. TapLoop tests NFC and QR functionality before every delivery.

FAQ

Which material is best for a sales team?

PVC usually works well for volume and practicality; metal can be added for team leaders.

Does the material affect NFC reading?

Every card is tested before delivery to make sure it reads correctly.
