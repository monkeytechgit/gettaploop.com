FAQ for teams

Questions before you get a quote

Can each team member have different information?+

Yes. Each profile can include a name, title, phone number, email, WhatsApp, social media, and its own links.

Do all the cards keep the same corporate design?+

Yes. We create a visual identity with your company's logo, colors, and style.

Can we combine PVC and metal cards?+

Yes. You can use PVC for sales teams and metal for executives or key roles.

Can we update titles or details later?+

Yes. Digital profiles can be updated without replacing the physical card.

Can new team members be added?+

Yes. We can produce additional cards while keeping the approved design.

How do we collect each team member's information?+

We guide you with a template or form to gather the details in an organized way.

Do the cards arrive programmed and ready to use?+

Yes. We program the NFC, link the QR code, verify profiles, and deliver everything ready to go.
