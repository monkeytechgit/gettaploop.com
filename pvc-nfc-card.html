FAQ

The most important things to know before requesting your Digital NFC Card.

What is an NFC digital business card?+

It's a physical PVC card with NFC technology and a QR code that opens a custom digital profile with your contact info, WhatsApp, social media, website, and other important links.

Does the other person need to install an app?+

No. The digital profile opens directly in the browser of a compatible phone when you tap the card or scan the QR code.

Can I customize the design of the NFC card?+

Yes. The card and digital profile are customized with your logo, name, title, colors, contact info, and main links.

Can I update my digital profile later?+

Yes. Depending on your plan, you can update your digital profile's info and links without replacing the physical card.

Does the card work with iPhone and Android?+

It works on NFC-compatible phones. The QR code also lets you open the same digital profile from the phone's camera.

How long does delivery take?+

Estimated delivery is 3-5 business days after design and production are approved. Timing can vary by quantity and destination.
