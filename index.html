Frequently asked questions about NFC cards

We answer the most common questions about our NFC cards and digital profiles.

What is an NFC digital business card?−

It's a physical card with an NFC chip and QR code that opens a digital profile for sharing contact info, WhatsApp, social media, your website, and other links.

Does the card need an app?−

No. The card works with NFC or QR on compatible devices, no app installation required.

What's included with the NFC card?+

It includes a custom card, a programmed NFC chip, a QR code, and a digital profile depending on the solution you choose.

Can I update my digital profile?+

Yes. Depending on the plan, you can update your info and links without replacing the card.

Can you make cards for my whole team?+

Yes. We create a corporate design and customize each team member's details.

How long does production take?+

Timing depends on quantity, material, and final design approval. We confirm it with every quote.
