Frequentlyasked questions

We answer the most common questions about how TapLoop works.

Does the other person need to install an app?+

No. The digital profile opens directly in the phone's browser via NFC or QR code.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?+

Yes. Compatible phones can open the profile with NFC, and the QR code works as a universal backup.

What happens if NFC doesn't work?+

The card also includes a QR code to open the same profile from the phone's camera.

Can I update my information later?+

Yes. You can update your digital profile's info and links without replacing the physical card.

Do NFC and QR open the same profile?+

Yes. Both options lead to the same TapLoop digital profile.

Can I use the card as many times as I want?+

Yes. You can share your profile as many times as you need.
