FAQ

NFC card and digital profile services

We answer questions about the design, programming, and delivery of TapLoop solutions.

What does TapLoop do?+

TapLoop designs, programs, and produces NFC digital business cards with a custom digital profile, QR code, and contact links for professionals, teams, and businesses.

What's included with an NFC digital business card?+

It includes a custom physical card, a programmed NFC chip, a linked QR code, and a digital profile with contact info, WhatsApp, email, website, social media, and other links.

Can the digital profile be customized with my brand?+

Yes. The profile and card are designed using your logo, colors, typography, details, and visual style, whether for an individual or a business.

Does TapLoop build solutions for teams?+

Yes. We can maintain a consistent visual identity while customizing each team member's name, title, phone number, email, photo, and links.

Does the other person need to install an app?+

No. The digital profile opens directly in the browser when you tap the card via NFC or scan the QR code.

Do the cards arrive ready to use?+

Yes. TapLoop programs, links, tests, and delivers the cards ready to share your digital profile.
