Questions about NFC cards

Frequently asked questions about NFC digital business cards

We answer the most common questions before you choose your PVC NFC card, metal card, or team solution.

What is an NFC digital business card? −

It's a physical card with NFC technology and a QR code that opens a custom digital profile with your contact info, WhatsApp, social media, website, location, and other important links.

What's the difference between a PVC NFC card and a metal one? +

The PVC card is lightweight, versatile, and great for everyday use, events, or teams. The metal card offers a more premium presentation, designed for executives, partners, and high-value contacts.

Do I need to install an app to use the NFC card? +

No. The digital profile opens directly in the phone's browser when you tap the card or scan the QR code.

Can I update my digital profile without changing the card? +

Yes. Depending on the plan, you can update your info, links, phone number, title, or photo without replacing the physical card.

Can you create NFC cards for my whole team? +

Yes. We design a corporate visual identity and customize each card and digital profile with every team member's details.
